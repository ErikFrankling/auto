# GPU digital evolution: research map, 2023–2026

Research date: 2026-10-31. **Measured** means reported experiments, not independently reproduced here; **demonstrated** includes qualitative examples; **claim** means an author's interpretation or ambition; **proposal** means my suggested experiment. Sources are primary papers, author sites, proceedings and repositories. No simulations were run.

## What is closest to the requested system?

**Synthesis:** EcoEvoJax, JaxLife and Harvard's ecology are the strongest academic starting points for inherited neural controllers, local reproduction and ecological selection. Flow-Lenia studies intrinsic selection in a different substrate; ASAL searches *world rules* using an external objective; Microcosmos supplies physics and controller optimization. These are materially different achievements. [EcoEvoJax](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09334), [JaxLife](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00853), [Harvard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18221), [Flow-Lenia](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08569), [ASAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17799), [Microcosmos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02954).

“No explicit fitness function” can mean no scalar ranking or optimizer: energy acquisition still changes reproductive success, and the resource/action rules define selection. It does not mean an absence of selection pressure or designer assumptions. This distinction is explicit in the ecological setup and is essential when comparing it with RL. [EcoEvoJax](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09334).

Fraser Price's scale, speed and evolving hierarchy remain **author claims**, supported here by the supplied notes rather than an independently inspectable implementation; his quoted ranges should not be read as a benchmark at every population/network combination. [Author's scale post](https://x.com/fraserpricee/status/2108837968353857800), [ecology thread](https://x.com/fraserpricee/status/2100357683060613259).

## People, systems and results

### Ecological neuroevolution

- **Gautier Hamon, Eleni Nisioti, Clément Moulin-Frier — EcoEvoJax (2023; GECCO Companion). Measured:** non-episodic JAX grid-world evolution, individual RNNs, physiology-dependent local births/deaths, continuously modified resources, and sustainable collective foraging. This is unusually close to the brief. It establishes adaptation and eco-evolutionary feedback, not indefinite innovation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09334), [author's proceedings bibliography](https://reytuag.github.io/gautier-hamon/data/thesis_hamon_gautier.pdf).
- **Reproducibility:** EcoEvoJax supplies pretrained populations from two independent seeds and scripts for laboratory evaluation and figure/statistical reproduction. Useful starting point for separating evolved traits from environmental effects; two supplied seeds are not strong evidence of universal robustness. [Repository](https://github.com/flowersteam/EcoEvoJax).
- **Chris Lu, Michael Beukman, Michael Matthews, Jakob Foerster — JaxLife (2024). Demonstrated:** evolved agriculture/terraforming, rudimentary communication and tool use in an energy-limited world with programmable robots. Attention encoders feed an LSTM; reproduction copies and perturbs weights without lifetime weight training. Some useful-machine examples and universal computation are *hand constructions*, distinct from spontaneous evolved achievements. Robots' energy-free operation is an engineered affordance. [Paper, §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.00853v1), [code](https://github.com/luchris429/JaxLife).
- **Joseph Bejjani, Aaron Walsman, Sham Kakade and collaborators — Harvard ecology (2025, revised December). Measured:** over 60,000 live agents in worlds exceeding one million cells; independent inherited MLP policies, not RNNs. Long-range resource mining, vision-based foraging and predation emerge. Larger worlds improve behavioral reliability. Appendix F shows initial headcount at fixed world size has little effect because populations converge toward carrying capacity; resource rules matter. Four seeds per setting and GPU nondeterminism limit certainty. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.18221v3).
- **Harvard benchmark:** Table 8 reports maximum allocated population 131,072 at 1024² cells; approximately 22k weights per policy; 55 steps/s Beach and 40 Ocean, with about 60 GB memory and approximately 10–14 hours per two-million-step run on one H100. Allocated slots differ from live population. These are reported measurements, not single-consumer-GPU promises. [Appendix E](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.18221v3#A5). Experimental scripts and the reusable engine are separate: [experiments](https://github.com/jbejjani2022/ecological-emergent-behavior), [dirt](https://github.com/aaronwalsman/dirt).

### Lenia, search for worlds, and embodied physics

- **Erwan Plantec, Hamon, Mayalen Etcheverry, Bert Chan, Pierre-Yves Oudeyer, Moulin-Frier — Flow-Lenia (ALIFE 2023; journal extension 2025). Measured/demonstrated:** conserved mass promotes localized patterns; localized, transported rule parameters permit competing kinds of patterns within one world. The extension analyzes evolutionary activity. An outer evolutionary strategy can also optimize locomotion. Intrinsic ecological dynamics and outer optimization must be reported separately. [Original](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.07906), [extension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08569), [author institution's account](https://radar.inria.fr/report/2024/flowers/index.html).
- **Hamon and FLOWERS collaborators — Sensorimotor Lenia. Measured:** diversity search, curriculum and gradient optimization discover self-maintaining moving patterns robust to obstacles and other perturbations; ablations identify contributions from those search components. Agency emerges at the pattern level from local CA rules, rather than a preassigned agent body. This is optimized sensorimotor self-organization, not unassisted Darwinian evolution. [Paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12577699/), [source](https://github.com/flowersteam/sensorimotor-lenia-search).
- **Akarsh Kumar, Chris Lu, Louis Kirsch, Yujin Tang, Kenneth Stanley, Phillip Isola, David Ha — Sakana ASAL (December 2024). Measured/demonstrated:** vision-language representations guide target search, temporal novelty search and diversity illumination across Lenia, Boids, Particle Life, Game of Life and NCA. New patterns and rule sets are shown. **Claim:** “open-ended” CA; the experiment operationalizes finite-window visual novelty, not unlimited hereditary adaptation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17799), [code](https://github.com/SakanaAI/asal).
- **ASAL++ (2025)** extends VLM-guided search and explicitly describes its aim as “open-ended-like” search. Relevant follow-up, but still external semantic guidance rather than organism-level natural selection. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22447).
- **Mark Tensen, Ciaran Regan, Chan, Mizuki Oka, Stanley, Grisha Szep — Microcosmos (2026). Measured/demonstrated:** elastic filaments in viscous 2D fluid; differentiable folding; neuroevolved swimming/chemotaxis; QD locomotion repertoires; linear particle-count scaling in reported benchmarks. **Claim/goal:** scalable physically grounded open-ended evolution. The release explicitly places birth, death and self-assembly among next steps; this is not yet a demonstrated naturally reproducing ecosystem. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02954), [code](https://github.com/alife-institute/microcosmos), [authors' release](https://staging.alife.institute/en/blog/microcosmos-release/).
- **Chris Heinemann — ALIEN:** a particularly relevant open-source CUDA particle/cell simulator with neural control, genomes, energy and self-replication facilities. **Implementation/author demonstrations**, not a controlled proof of OEE; inspect it for bonding, construction and evolution mechanics. CUDA is a practical mismatch for the AMD GPU identified in the supplied environment notes. [Source and requirements](https://github.com/chrxh/alien).

### Open-endedness: what has actually been established?

- **Stanley, L. B. Soros and Joel Lehman:** the foundational strand emphasizes novelty, stepping stones, minimal criteria and endogenous opportunities rather than progress toward one predefined optimum. Soros/Stanley's Chromaria controls support proposed necessary conditions *within that model*: nontrivial reproduction criteria, organisms creating new opportunities, agent-directed interaction, and representations capable of increasing complexity. This is not a general sufficiency theorem. [Chromaria workshop report](https://workshops.alife.org/oee4/papers/soros-oee4-camera-ready.pdf), [ICML tutorial](https://icml.cc/media/icml-2019/Slides/4336.pdf).
- **Hughes, Dennis, Parker-Holder, Behbahani and collaborators (ICML 2024 position paper):** define observer-relative open-endedness through both novelty and learnability, excluding mere unstructured surprise. **Conceptual proposal**, not a new million-agent evolution result. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04268).
- **Assessment:** restricted and finite-horizon forms of OEE have empirical support; there is no result among the systems reviewed here establishing indefinite, progressively adaptive, ecosystem-level innovation comparable to biological evolution. ASAL's visual novelty, Leniabreeder's diversity and ecological adaptation answer different questions. A finite computer run also cannot demonstrate literal infinity. [ASAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17799), [Leniabreeder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04235), [definitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04268).
- **Rui Wang, Joel Lehman, Jeff Clune, Kenneth Stanley — POET/Enhanced POET (heritage, 2019–2020). Measured:** co-generation of environments and solutions, cross-environment transfer and stepping stones yield challenges/solutions unavailable to direct optimization baselines. It uses externally evaluated tasks and policy optimization, not birth/death ecology. Its transferable lesson is to preserve stepping stones and allow changing challenges. [POET](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.01753), [Enhanced POET](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.08536).
- **Maxence Faldor, Antoine Cully — Leniabreeder (ALIFE 2024). Measured:** QD discovers diverse Lenia patterns using hand-designed or learned descriptors. **Claim:** empirical evidence suggesting unbounded diversity; continued diversity over tested runs is weaker than proof of unbounded innovation. MAP-Elites/AURORA maintain repertoires through an external archive, which can be used to search world parameters without directly imposing organism fitness. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04235), [code](https://github.com/maxencefaldor/Leniabreeder).

### Adjacent work worth borrowing from

- **Neural MMO 2.0 — Joseph Suarez, Ryan Sullivan and collaborators (NeurIPS 2023):** multi-agent, multi-task RL with survival, resources and combat. Policies learn through RL objectives; this is a rich ecology-inspired benchmark, not millions of independent naturally selected genomes. Borrow observations, evaluations and social-interaction design. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03736), [proceedings supplement](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/9ca22870ae0ba55ee50ce3e2d269e5de-Supplemental-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf).
- **Mordvintsev, Randazzo, Niklasson, Levin and collaborators — NCA:** shared local neural rules can be trained to grow and repair target patterns. A pixel usually carries state, not its own heritable neural network. Growth, robustness and regeneration are real demonstrations; target-loss training is not ecological evolution. [Foundational experiment and code](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/).
- **Miotti, Niklasson, Randazzo, Mordvintsev — Differentiable Logic CA (2025):** train relaxed logic circuits and discretize at inference; demonstrated Game of Life rule learning and robust pattern generation. Interesting route to inexpensive discrete controllers, but not an evolved ecosystem. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04912), [authors' implementation/explanation](https://google-research.github.io/self-organising-systems/difflogic-ca/).
- **Faldor/Cully — CAX (ICLR 2025):** accelerator-native JAX CA library with discrete, continuous and neural examples; useful substrate and baseline implementation. Its acceleration results are benchmark-specific and do not establish feasible million-agent independent-network throughput. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02651), [code/proceedings citation](https://github.com/maxencefaldor/cax).
- **Blaise Agüera y Arcas, Jyrki Alakuijala, James Evans, Ben Laurie, Mordvintsev, Niklasson, Randazzo, Luca Versari — Computational Life/BFF (2024). Measured:** random self-modifying programs generate self-replicators without an explicit fitness landscape, including experiments without background mutation; interactions themselves alter code. Some languages support replicators yet did not spontaneously generate them in experiments. This separates emergence of replication from evolution starting with a designed replicator. No neural controller is required. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19108).
- **Tierra/Avida heritage:** Tierra demonstrated replication competition and parasites; Avida experimentally traced complex rewarded logic functions through simpler stepping stones. Neither is a proof that unconstrained complexity necessarily keeps growing. Avida's task rewards are a crucial qualification. [Ray's ecology discussed in an experimental digital-ecology paper](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1002928), [Lenski/Ofria/Pennock/Adami experiment](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature01568).
- **Evolution Gym — Bhatia and collaborators (NeurIPS 2021, heritage):** co-design of voxel bodies and controllers across task benchmarks; useful for testing morphology/control coupling, but task rewards and episodic optimization differ from endogenous selection. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/file/118921efba23fc329e6560b27861f0c2-Paper.pdf).
- **Plasticity/meta-learning — Giannakakis, Khajehabdollahi, Levina (2024):** evolved local learning rules in foraging agents depend strongly on network bottlenecks and task structure. This supports testing inherited plasticity as an alternative to evolving fixed weights alone, not a universal claim that plastic networks improve OEE. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13649). **Indirect encoding heritage:** Najarro, Sudhakaran, Glanois, Risi's HyperNCA grows task-solving networks through a developmental rule. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11674).
- **Moreno, Rodriguez Papa, Ofria — DISHTINY (2024):** a digital multicellularity case study reports ten distinct morphologies, some with asymmetric growth/life stages; novelty, complexity and adaptation sometimes diverge. Particularly relevant evidence against using one “interestingness” score. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07241).

## Design knobs: evidence versus hypotheses

| Knob | What evidence supports; what to test |
|---|---|
| Population and space | Separate actual sustained population, world area, density and initial headcount. Larger space can create refuges and travel opportunities; bigger initial populations need not persist. **Proposal:** connected world versus isolated patches with equal total compute/resources. [Harvard ablations](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.18221v3#A6) |
| Direct weights | Simplest faithful baseline: independent inherited small controllers and asexual mutations. JaxLife demonstrates viability; it also notes crossover difficulties in fixed-topology networks. **Proposal:** begin with copy-plus-perturb, then compare sparse versus dense perturbations at matched expected change. [JaxLife §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.00853v1#S3) |
| Indirect genomes/hypernetworks | Developmental encodings can generate useful networks from shared local rules. This is evidence of expressive compression, not superior open-ended ecology. **Proposal:** compare equal genome bytes and equal simulation time; generated controller storage can still dominate memory. [HyperNCA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11674) |
| Shared-species weights | **Proposal/tradeoff:** storing one controller per genotype saves duplication, but tying all members to a species policy changes individual heredity unless offspring can create new genotypes. Compare clone-deduplicated immutable genomes against mutable shared policies. The NCA shared-rule model illustrates why shared rules and independent organisms are distinct abstractions. [NCA](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/) |
| Mutation operators | Mutation size/rate and neural architecture require sweeps; do not import a single “optimal” rate across substrates. Self-modification can generate variation even without background random mutation. **Proposal:** sparse weight changes, inherited mutation rate, occasional duplication/pruning, with matched controls. [BFF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19108), [Harvard mutation ablation](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.18221v3#A6.S4) |
| Speciation | External niches/archives preserve diversity in QD, but those are algorithmic protections. Spatial/ecological segregation is different. **Proposal:** measure lineages first; compare optional reproductive barriers or protected niches against purely local competition. [Leniabreeder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04235) |
| Energy/resources | Continuously renewed, spatial resources plus physiological reproduction support adaptive foraging. **Proposal:** track conservation/source/sink budgets; separate energy flux from recyclable biomass; sweep costs before adding actions. Energy-rich trivial reproduction and resource starvation are diagnostic failure modes, not signs of low intelligence. [EcoEvoJax](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09334) |
| Predation | Host–parasite coevolution can increase complexity/evolvability in controlled Avida experiments; this does not imply arbitrary combat always helps. **Proposal:** compare edible corpses, costly attack and defense with no-predation controls; watch extinction and collapse of prey diversity. [Zaman et al. experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4267771/) |
| Bonding/multicellularity | Distinct morphology, adaptation and complexity can evolve independently. **Proposal:** require inherited group reproduction, persistent division of labor and a group-level advantage before calling a bonded swarm a major transition; test bond removal and cell mixing. [DISHTINY](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07241) |
| Keeping novelty going | Evidence favors preserving stepping stones, endogenous environmental modification and continuing opportunities; none guarantees unlimited novelty. **Proposal:** resource transformations, persistent environmental memory, dispersal/refuges and evolvable interaction channels, each added separately. [POET](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.01753), [Chromaria](https://workshops.alife.org/oee4/papers/soros-oee4-camera-ready.pdf) |

**Stall diagnosis (inference):** distinguish extinction, fixation, resource equilibration, cycling among existing strategies, neutral genome drift and a representation ceiling. More particles cannot repair an action space with no new ecological opportunities. Test replayed ancestors, mutated descendants and environment manipulations rather than judging animation alone. This follows the distinction between adaptation, novelty and ecological potential in [MODES](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00280) and the experimental divergence of these properties in [DISHTINY](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07241).

## Measuring whether anything interesting is happening

1. **Basic ecology — proposed minimum logger:** births/deaths and causes; population/age/energy distributions; actual action use; resource flux; occupancy and spatial correlations; attack success and trophic transfers. Normalize by live agents and elapsed generations, not only GPU ticks. Harvard's engine already exposes movement/eating, biomass use and attack/homicide measurements. [Engine](https://github.com/aaronwalsman/dirt).
2. **Evolutionary activity statistics:** persistent components gain activity as they survive selection; compare with a matched neutral/shadow process to distinguish selected persistence from chance. Analyze diversity, accumulated activity and new activity together. The choice of component (gene, controller cluster, behavioral trait) changes the interpretation. Flow-Lenia applies this tradition to its own dynamics. [Flow-Lenia extension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08569).
3. **MODES — Dolson, Vostinar, Wiser, Ofria (2019):** a toolbox for **change, novelty, complexity and ecological potential**, with algorithms and C++ implementations, evaluated on NK landscapes and Avida. Adapt its ancestry/novelty logic rather than presenting an arbitrary entropy curve as “MODES.” It diagnoses hallmarks, not a universal OEE certificate. [Journal paper](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00280), [author manuscript](https://cse.msu.edu/~dolsonem/pdfs/modes_paper.pdf).
4. **ASAL visual novelty:** the source computes frame-embedding dot products, masks to earlier frames and averages the maximum similarity; minimizing this favors difference from the nearest earlier representation. Illumination instead compares simulations. **Proposed safeguards:** frozen encoder, fixed rendering, held-out longer horizons, alternative views/embeddings and noise controls. Color changes, camera changes or visual cycles can confound ecological meaning. [Actual scoring code](https://github.com/SakanaAI/asal/blob/main/asal_metrics.py).
5. **Genomes/phenotypes — proposed:** keep parent IDs, birth time, mutation summary and immutable genome hashes; sample genome snapshots. Cluster both parameter distance and behavior in a standardized assay. Neural permutation symmetries and neutral drift make weight-distance clusters poor biological “species” by themselves; JaxLife's crossover discussion highlights representation alignment issues. [JaxLife](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.00853v1).
6. **Phylogeny — proposed:** ancestry depth, branch survival, turnover, clade abundance and phylogenetic diversity, then compare with neutral controls. Long branches can signify persistence rather than improvement; inspect innovations on the line of descent and replay them. MODES is a methodological anchor for ancestry-based analysis. [MODES](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00280).
7. **Adaptation/ecological function — proposed:** cross-play ancestors and descendants; transplant into common resource conditions; remove predators, signals, bonds or environmental products. Retained performance across assays is stronger evidence than attractive motion. Score novelty, complexity and adaptation separately. [DISHTINY case study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07241), [EcoEvoJax laboratory evaluations](https://github.com/flowersteam/EcoEvoJax).
8. **Assembly theory:** Sharma, Czégel, Lachmann, Kempes, Walker, Cronin (2023) combine assembly history/minimal construction with copy abundance to characterize selection. **Proposal:** apply only after defining discrete objects, reusable components and assembly operations. A floating-point RNN's size is not automatically its assembly index. It is an exploratory lens, not a validated neural-ecosystem OEE metric; its broad interpretation is contested. [Original](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06600-9), [published critique](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10978598/).
9. **Learnability — proposed:** train a small predictive observer on earlier behavioral descriptors; evaluate future prediction and whether additional history improves it. Pair sustained surprise with improving learning to separate structured novelty from random flicker. Report dependence on the observer. [Hughes et al. definition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04268).

**Reporting protocol (proposal):** save seeds/configuration/code revision, allocated slots versus living agents, actual genome storage, wall time and generations. Use independent runs and uncertainty bands; extend horizons before claiming sustained novelty. Preserve random-controller, frozen-genome and neutral-inheritance controls. Report all extinctions and failed seeds. This addresses the run variability documented by [Harvard](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.18221v3#A5) and the need for multiple hallmarks in [MODES](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00280).

## Most promising one-GPU experiments

These are **proposals**, not claims of priority: the reviewed literature motivates them, but does not establish that nobody has tried them.

1. **Best first project: controlled spatial ecology.** Small inherited RNNs, local reproduction, energy and recyclable corpses; compare one connected arena, disconnected islands and tunable dispersal. Hold total resources and agent-update budget constant. Ask whether refuge-mediated coexistence sustains *adaptive* novelty longer than simply raising headcount. Start from [EcoEvoJax](https://github.com/flowersteam/EcoEvoJax) and the scale caveats in [Harvard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18221).
2. **Resource transformations as endogenous opportunities.** Introduce a few substances whose waste products can feed other agents; charge for production and movement. Measure evolving cross-feeding networks and test removal of producers. This directly tests opportunity creation, rather than adding an explicit novelty reward. Motivation: [Chromaria](https://workshops.alife.org/oee4/papers/soros-oee4-camera-ready.pdf), [digital ecological networks](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1002928).
3. **Evolve plasticity under ecological selection.** Compare fixed RNN weights with inherited local Hebbian/neuromodulated updates under seasonal/resource reversals; charge memory/plasticity compute equally. Use lifetime adaptation tests, reset hidden states separately from learned weights, and distinguish inherited learning rules from inherited learned values. Motivation: [2024 foraging/plasticity study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13649).
4. **Cheap genomes without forced policy sharing.** Deduplicate identical inherited controllers, then compare direct weights with a compact developmental encoding at equal bytes/time. Allow new genotypes on division and track lost/gained niches. Motivation: [HyperNCA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11674); no reviewed study establishes the winning encoding for this exact ecology.
5. **Bonding with an ecological reason to exist.** Add a transport/feeding tradeoff that groups can exploit, while allowing cheating and fragmentation. Compare aggregation with heritable group life cycles and test reproducible division of labor. Start only after a stable single-cell baseline. Motivation: [DISHTINY](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07241), [ALIEN implementation](https://github.com/chrxh/alien).
6. **Search the universe, then leave it alone.** Use a small QD/ASAL outer search over resource/cost/diffusion parameters; freeze selected worlds and run ecological selection on held-out seeds/horizons. Rank worlds by multiple *held-out* measures, not just training CLIP novelty. Motivation: [ASAL source](https://github.com/SakanaAI/asal), [Leniabreeder](https://github.com/maxencefaldor/Leniabreeder).

**Practical scope:** prioritize independent replicated worlds and instrumentation before maximum population. Raw float32 controller storage is `4 × population × parameters` bytes: one million agents × 256 weights is 1.024 GB; ×10,000 is 40 GB, before state, fields and buffers (**arithmetic**, not measured performance). For Erik's AMD setup, WGSL is the existing project proposal; CUDA-specific ALIEN is not directly portable. JAX projects require a compatible accelerator backend and should be benchmarked on the actual machine rather than extrapolated from H100 results. [Project source requirements](https://github.com/chrxh/alien), [JAX installation/backend documentation](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/installation.html).

**Suggested reading order:** EcoEvoJax → Harvard plus ablations → Flow-Lenia journal extension → JaxLife → ASAL scoring source → MODES → DISHTINY → Microcosmos. Together they distinguish ecological selection, metrics, representational opportunity and scalable physics; links appear above.
