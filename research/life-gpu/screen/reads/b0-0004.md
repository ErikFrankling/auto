**1. Life in this set**
Duller than baseline. 12 of 16 worlds live; t000, t002, t003, t011 sit at 1-7 cells. Live median 2,068 cells (baseline 16,288; our best, 3,681, is under its lowest, 5,030). Species at end about 1 in nine worlds (baseline 7.5), new species median 2 (15.5), nearly all strategies ground-eating "roaming grazers". Energy from other cells 4.5% (8%). Moving 35% (17%). Turnover 0.15, same as baseline. Typically a founder strategy is replaced by a more mobile one (t007 moving 30 to 42%, t012 23 to 33).

**2. What the maps add**
Seen: in all 12, cells live on a ring along the outer wall; the centre is empty and barely walked, even where interior sun is bright (t009, t010, t012, t014). Sun doesn't track cells (t009: highest sun 0.184, only 751 cells). Inferred: something at the rim, maybe mineral, limits life. Dense, still, single-colour clumps sit at the wall in t006 (two), t008, t010, t012. t008's tracks panel shows faint straight diagonals; too small to confirm roads.

**3. Candidates**
1. **t008.** Only real predation: 24% of energy from other cells, 14% from biting (baseline 8%, 0.7%), rising all run. Omnivore strategy 0 to 76% (bite 16%, meat 10%); flows bite 266, meat 189, ground 1,426. Map: still kin blob top-left, movers round the ring. Inferred: one lineage turned omnivore, not yet plateaued. Check: victim genome distance per bite; with about 2 species, bites may mostly hit kin (cannibalism).
2. **t009.** 12 species seen, 5 strategies at 6-47% at the end, 9 brain groups, dimensions 7.6 (set max), turnover 0.29 (set max). Inferred: drift at 751 cells may explain it. Check: shares jump (57, 8, 14, 6, 19); sample finer. A random walk means nothing real.


**4. Problems**
- t000 goes 0 then 3 cells, t011 0 then 1; a world can't restart from zero unless something reseeds or the count is wrong.
- t009: 5 strategies above 6%, but fine clusters say 1 above 3%.
- "Species explain behaviour 0.000" with one species is meaningless, yet counts as a significant baseline test.
