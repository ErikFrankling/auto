**1. Life in this set**
Duller than baseline. Seen: all worlds are sessile ground-feeders. Median at end: 33k cells (baseline 16k); moving 0.5% (16.6%); resting 66% (2.3%, max 23%); energy from other cells 4% (8%); biting 0-2% (0.7%). Each world lists two strategies, a falling sitting/slow grazer replaced by a rising resting grazer, both 87-98% ground. Lasting clusters median 2 (same), species 10.6 (7.5). Inferred: crowded, no pressure to diversify; the handover looks like ageing (resting mean age 4,000-9,000 vs 1,500-3,500), not a new strategy.

**3. Candidates**
