# b0-0015 (walls, weather_sun)

**1. Life in this set**
Seen: nearly every world is two grazer types eating 88-98% ground: roamers (move 20-40%) and sitters (0-10%, live 2-3x longer). Against baseline: cells median ~10.1k vs 16.3k; species ~5.9 vs 7.5; moving 0.29 vs 0.17; food from other cells 0.052 vs 0.103; bites 0.004 vs 0.012. Lasting strategies median 2, as control. Biting is 0% of energy in 12 of 16 worlds. So: duller on predation, same on variety, more roaming. In t003, t006, t009, t010 roamers replace sitters.

**2. What the maps add**
Seen: sitters are dense single-colour blocks; roamers a thin dust everywhere. Block edges often run straight along the wall grid (t001, t004, t007, t011); genome colours meet at wall lines in t001 and t004. The grid is the same in all 16 worlds, so probably generated. No thin trail lines; no hunters at colony edges. Infer: walls keep lineages apart; not new lifestyles.
