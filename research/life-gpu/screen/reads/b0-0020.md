# b0-0020 (coat, coat_sense, mineral, walls, weather_sun)

**1. Life in this set**
Duller and smaller than baseline. 4 of 16 worlds never got going (t000, t003, t006, t014; baseline 0 of 32). The 12 live worlds hold 1,122-4,566 cells (median ~2,300; baseline median 16,288, lowest 5,030). Everyone grazes: 97-99% ground; energy from other cells 1-2% (baseline 8%), biting 0% (0.7%); no group hunting. Species at end median ~1.6 (baseline 7.5); five worlds have exactly 1. Births are mineral-limited (74% mineral only, 6% energy only), probably capping populations. One repeatable change: moving climbs from 5-16% to 21-34% in all 12 worlds as founding slow grazers fall from 50-100% to under 6% in seven. Density is flat, so not crowding. Brain dims 3.4-7.8 (baseline 3.3) look like drift.

**2. What the maps add**
Seen: in all 12 live worlds cells hug the border and thin rock lines, leaving interiors and the brightest sun empty. No red or blue diet anywhere. "Traffic on lines" (124-373 vs 57-139) peaks in t007 and t004, both with 0 colonies: cells along walls, not trails. Kin stay apart by region in t008, t010, t011, t013. Dense sitting kin blobs at rock edges: t009, t011, t013, t015. Inferred: border-hugging is a mineral or wall effect, not a lifestyle.

**3. Candidates** (weak; none is a new way of life)
1. t010: only world where species explain behaviour (0.507; rest at most 0.21); four strategies held all run (16-33%); kin colours separate by corner. Inferred: geography, not coexistence. Check: 30x30 blocks each holding several strategies would refute it.
2. t008: 4,566 cells, 13 behaviour clusters none above 28%, 9 colonies (largest 139). Inferred: drift (explain 0.076). Check: move 100 cells between regions; equal energy means drift.
3. t015: colony of 231; gifts stay 2-3% of food all run (others fade to 0-1%). Check: equal gifts per cell outside colonies means noise.
