# b0-0013 (age_metabolic, birth_choice, gang, mineral, plants, waste)

**1. Life in this set.** Duller than baseline. t014 and t015 are dead. The other 14 hold 1.3k-9.0k cells (median 3.6k; control 16.3k, min 5.0k). Births are mineral-limited (61% mineral, 13% energy). Nearly all are ground grazers: energy from other cells is 0-4% in 10 worlds (control median 8%); only t004 17%, t001 14%, t003 10%, t010 7%. Species at end median 2.25 (control 7.5); species explain behaviour ~0.15 (control 0.55). Turnover is higher (~0.22 vs 0.14): one grazer replaces another; gang goes unused. In the 11 worlds with <=4% biting, moving rises from 18-34% to 39-50% (control max 37.5%) even as cells grow (t000, t002, t006, t008, t013).

**2. What the maps add.** Seen: genome colours sit in regional patches (t004, t008, t010, t012). Cells follow sun, edges and rock walls. Biters sit: 60-70% of biting-diet pixels are sitters (t001, t004, t010, t013), in dense edge clumps of one narrow kin colour (t004 sd 0.04-0.09 vs 0.11-0.13 overall). No thin trails.

**3. Candidates.**
1. **t004.** Bites/cell 1 to 9% and other-cell food 4 to 16% over the run, while cells held ~6k from 60k ticks (not crowding). Four omnivore clusters (19-28% bite diet, moving 19-25% vs grazers 29-43%) appear 60k-105k. Biting is 10% of energy (control median 0.7%, max 20%); species explain behaviour 0.46. Infer: one lineage evolved sit-and-bite and is spreading. Within baseline range. Check: if the four clusters trace to different ancestors, I'm wrong.
2. **t001.** Biggest (9,035 cells), 7 colonies (largest 534), 7% biting. Cells and bites rose together (5.3k to 9.0k; 3 to 8%): maybe crowding. Check: bite rate of edge-clump vs interior cells at equal density.
3. **t003 (weak).** Cell-average food from others 20% (set max); ~600 meat/gift-diet cells, 20-40x the no-bite worlds, 61% moving. Infer: roaming scavengers. Check: near fresh corpses, or newborns carrying a parental gift?
