## 1. Life in this set
Seen: same as baseline. Medians, set vs baseline: cells 16.6k vs 16.3k, species 7.0 vs 7.5, new species 15.5 vs 15.5, turnover 0.15 vs 0.14, moving 15% vs 17%. Strategies eat 90-97% ground. Biting median 2% of energy (baseline 0.7%); t000 (20%) and t015 (17%) reach the baseline top. No group hunting (90% of bites solo), no following. In 6 worlds the founder strategy bites 3-15% and is gone by 60k. A "slow omnivore" (26-30% bites) appears in t000, t003, t014.

## 2. What the maps add
Seen: no trails anywhere (t000 has the highest traffic on lines, 133, no thin lines). Every map shows dense still blocks on bright sun (t009: two blocks, two sun spots) plus a thin roaming scatter on dark ground. t006, t011, t012, t013 blocks are clonal with sharp genome borders. In t000 the biters are the diffuse salmon northern cloud; the corner colonies are pure grazers.

## 3. Candidates
1. **t000.** Seen: omnivore share 0,0,1,5,5,27,39,52%; bites per cell 2% to 17%; 20% of energy from biting, still rising. Infer: biting re-evolved after the founders' faded. Check: tally bites by victim genome cluster; mostly salmon kin means not predation.
2. **t015.** Seen: biting 4% to 12% per cell inside the 79% roaming strategy; 17% biting; lowest turnover (0.08). Mixed diet sits in the olive and pink patches, not the cyan block. Infer: slow drift to omnivory. Check: victims mostly roamers, not colony cells, means colonies aren't prey.
3. **t007.** Seen: 8% biting; species-explain-behaviour 0.75 (highest); dark-green genome patch with brown mixed diet at the pink colony's sun hotspot. Infer: possible invader lineage. Check: if its diet and age match the pink neighbours, drop it.

## 4. Problems
Per-cell "from other cells" and "bites" disagree with strategy diets in quiet worlds (t009 30%/7% vs about 7%/2%; t004 bites 10% vs 1%). "Bites across strategies" is 0.54 in t011 (0% biting) and 0.02 in t015 (strategies bite 3-15%). t011/t012 colony speed is 16 squares per 10k ticks vs 3-7 elsewhere on fixed sun patches: likely a tracking artefact.
